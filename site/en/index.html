Features

Let Minutes take the minutes.

Minutes records the meeting app’s audio and your own voice. When the meeting ends, it transcribes and summarizes with the services you choose. It lives in the menu bar and waits for your next meeting.

Live captions. Captions during the meeting are made on your Mac with macOS speech recognition, in Japanese or English. You can switch while recording.

Summary, decisions, and action items.

Transcript and summary. After the meeting, Minutes writes a transcript with speakers, a summary, decisions, action items, and open questions — each linked back to what was said. English meetings are summarized in English (or in Japanese, if you prefer).

Privacy

You decide what leaves your Mac, per meeting.

Each meeting is either “cloud OK” or “local only”. Audio and minutes are stored on your Mac.

Cloud OK

Sent to the services you choose

Recorded audio
Sent after the meeting to the transcription service you chose (ElevenLabs or OpenAI), with attendee names and terms to improve accuracy.
Transcript, title, attendees
Sent to the summarizer you chose (Codex: OpenAI; Claude Code and the Anthropic API: Anthropic).
Export and sync
Copied to the folder you chose.

Local only

Nothing leaves your Mac

Recorded audio
Never sent. Transcribed on your Mac.
Transcript, title, attendees
Never sent. No summary is made.
Export and sync
Not exported.
  • Live captions are always made on your Mac.
  • No telemetry. This page loads no analytics either.
  • API keys are stored in the Keychain.
  • Minutes checks GitHub for a new version once a day (you can turn this off).
  • On first use, Minutes downloads macOS speech models (from Apple) and speaker diarization models (from Hugging Face).
  • Audio is deleted after 30 days by default (only for exported meetings). Transcripts and minutes are kept.

You are responsible for telling attendees that you are recording and for obtaining any consent required by law or your organization. Minutes can copy a notice text for you at any time.

Before you start

What you need

Mac

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon
  • macOS 26 or later
  • The app’s interface is in Japanese.

Transcription (one of)

  • ElevenLabs Scribe v2 (recommended, API key)
  • OpenAI (API key)
  • On your Mac (no key, lower accuracy)

Summary (one of)

  • Codex (ChatGPT sign-in)
  • Claude Code (Claude sign-in)
  • Anthropic API (API key)
  • No summary
